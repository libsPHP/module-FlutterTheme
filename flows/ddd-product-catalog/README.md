## What This Feature Does

The Product Catalog allows you to browse, search, and explore all products in the Magento store. You can view product details, images, prices, and reviews, filter by category and price, and discover related products.

**Key Benefits**:
- Browse thousands of products with easy pagination
- Fast search to find exactly what you need
- Filter by category, price range, and attributes
- View detailed product information and reviews
- See related products for complementary items

---

## How It Works

**In Simple Terms:**

Think of the catalog like a smart supermarket:

1. **Browsing** = Walking through aisles
   - Products organized in categories (like aisles)
   - Each shelf shows similar items
   - Clear price tags and product info

2. **Search** = Asking store assistant
   - Tell them what you want
   - They bring you matching items
   - Fast and accurate

3. **Filtering** = Using filters on a website
   - Set your price range
   - Choose specific brand
   - Select features you need
   - Only matching items shown

4. **Product Details** = Reading product packaging
   - Full description
   - Ingredients/materials
   - Reviews from other buyers
   - Related products

## Quick Example

### Example: Finding a Laptop

**Goal**: Find a laptop between $500-$1000

**Steps**:
1. Open the app
2. Tap "Categories" → "Electronics" → "Laptops"
3. Tap "Filter"
4. Set price range: $500 - $1000
5. Tap "Apply"
6. Browse filtered results
7. Tap a laptop to see details
8. Read reviews and specifications
9. Check related accessories

**Result**: You found the perfect laptop in your budget!

## Common Questions

### How many products can I view?
You can browse the entire store catalog - thousands of products if available. Products load in pages (20-50 at a time) for fast performance.

### Can I search by product SKU?
Yes! Enter the SKU in the search bar to find a specific product.

### Are product images real?
Yes, all images are actual product photos from the Magento store.

### How often are prices updated?
Prices update in real-time from the Magento store. You always see current prices.

### Can I read reviews before buying?
Yes! Product details pages include customer reviews and ratings.

### What if a product is out of stock?
Out-of-stock products are marked clearly. You may be able to get notified when they're back (depends on store settings).
