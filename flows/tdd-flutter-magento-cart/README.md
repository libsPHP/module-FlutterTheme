## What This Feature Does

The Shopping Cart allows you to collect products you want to purchase, adjust quantities, and proceed to checkout. It works like a physical shopping cart - you can add items, remove them, or change quantities before buying.

**Key Benefits**:
- Add products from anywhere in the app
- Adjust quantities anytime
- Cart saves automatically - continue later
- Works even without an account (guest checkout)

---

## How It Works

**In Simple Terms:**

Think of it like a physical shopping cart:

1. **Adding Items** = Putting products in your cart
   - Browse products
   - Tap "Add to Cart"
   - Item appears in your cart

2. **Viewing Cart** = Checking what's in your cart
   - Tap cart icon
   - See all items with quantities
   - See total price

3. **Updating** = Changing your mind
   - Increase/decrease quantity
   - Remove items you don't want
   - Total updates automatically

4. **Checkout** = Going to the cashier
   - Tap "Checkout"
   - Enter shipping info
   - Pay and complete order

## Quick Example

### Example: Adding Products to Cart

**Goal**: Add 2 laptops to cart

**Steps**:
1. Browse to "Laptops" category
2. Find the laptop you want
3. Select quantity: 2
4. Tap "Add to Cart"
5. You'll see: "Item added to cart!"
6. Cart icon shows: 2 items

**Result**: 2 laptops are now in your cart, ready for checkout!

---

### Example: Updating Cart

**Goal**: Change quantity from 2 to 1

**Steps**:
1. Tap cart icon
2. Find the laptop in your cart
3. Tap "−" to decrease quantity to 1
4. Total updates automatically

**Result**: Cart now has 1 laptop, total price adjusted.

---

### Example: Removing Items

**Goal**: Remove an item from cart

**Steps**:
1. Open cart
2. Find the item to remove
3. Tap "Remove" or trash icon
4. Item disappears, total updates

**Result**: Item removed from cart.

## Common Questions

### Does my cart save if I close the app?
**Yes!** Your cart automatically saves. You can close the app and come back later - your items will still be there.

### Can I shop without an account?
**Yes!** You can add items to cart and checkout as a guest. Creating an account lets you save cart across devices.

### What happens if I log in with items in my guest cart?
Your guest cart merges with your account cart. You won't lose any items!

### Is there a limit to how many items I can add?
You can add as many as you want, but some products may have stock limits. If you try to add more than available, you'll see a message.

### Can I save my cart for later?
Yes! Your cart saves automatically. Items stay in your cart until you remove them or complete the purchase.

### Do prices in cart update if they change?
Prices update when you refresh the cart. Final prices are confirmed at checkout.
